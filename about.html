ABOUT US

INTRODUCTION

What is college diaries?
CollegeDiaries is a student-built project where students share their college experiences, stories, and memories. It brings students together and celebrates every unique journey. 💚

OUR MISSION

Why did students create this?

Students created this project to learn team work and github,and to give everyone a place to share their college life in their own words.

OUR COMMUNITY

Who is this project for?

This project is for college students,classmates and anyone who wants to remember and share their student journey.

OUR VISION

What do we hope to achieve?

We hope to build a friendly space where every student has a profile, every story is heard,and we all learn to build the website together.
